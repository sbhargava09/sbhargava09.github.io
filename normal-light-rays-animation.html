How a healthy eye focuses light

Drawn to scale: 22 mm axial length, 21 mm globe, 11.7 mm cornea, 4.5 × 9.5 mm lens. A set of rays enters together, passes through the pupil, refracts at the centre of the lens, and lands in the foveal pit — a dip 1.5 mm wide and 0.17 mm deep. The screen then clears completely before the next set enters.
