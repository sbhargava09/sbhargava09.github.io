The iridocorneal angle

Proportioned from published biometry: lens 9.5 mm (equator) × 4.5 mm (axial), anterior chamber depth ~3 mm, ciliary body (pars plicata) ~2 mm, iris root–to–margin ~4.5 mm. The trabecular meshwork (~0.6 mm) is drawn slightly larger than true scale so it stays legible — true to scale it'd be a hairline.
