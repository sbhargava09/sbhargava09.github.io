How cataract surgery restores clear vision

We are born with a clean lens in our eyes. This lens focuses light onto the retina, allowing us to see. With age, our lens turns dirty, and the rays scatter, causing decrease in vision. Another name for the dirty lens is “cataract”. In cataract surgery, we take the dirty lens out, and place a new permanent lens to re-focus the light rays to the retina.
