Concept preview — angle-based MIGS

Zooming in on the drainage angle

Fluid is produced by the ciliary processes, behind the iris. It flows forward, through a narrow gap between the iris and the lens. At the iridocorneal angle, it drains outward through the trabecular meshwork. That angle — the corner where the iris meets the cornea — is where angle-based surgery does its work. Let’s look closer. Aqueous crosses the trabecular meshwork into Schlemm’s canal, then leaves the eye through collecting channels.
