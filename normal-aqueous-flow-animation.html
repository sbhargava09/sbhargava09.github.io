Where the eye drains its fluid

Aqueous fluid is produced inside the eye and fills the space behind the cornea. Look closely at the corner where the cornea meets the iris — the iridocorneal angle. Zooming into that angle… Fluid is produced by the ciliary processes, behind the iris. It flows forward, through a narrow gap between the iris and the lens. At the iridocorneal angle, it drains outward through the trabecular meshwork.
