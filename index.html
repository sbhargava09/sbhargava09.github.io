About

A physician who believes patients deserve clarity.

“I want every patient to leave better informed about their eyes than when they arrived.”

Dr. Siddharth Bhargava is a fellowship-trained ophthalmologist with specialized expertise in glaucoma management, cataract surgery, premium intraocular lens counseling, and complex anterior segment care. He completed his medical degree at Albany Medical College and his ophthalmology residency at Eastern Virginia Medical School.

He then completed a glaucoma fellowship at Washington University in St. Louis, where he focused on medical and surgical glaucoma, micro-invasive glaucoma surgery, and the intersection of glaucoma care with cataract and lens-based surgery.

Dr. Bhargava speaks English, Hindi, and Urdu and is an active member of the American Glaucoma Society and the American Society of Cataract and Refractive Surgery.

Clinical expertise

Areas of focus

From routine annual exams to complex glaucoma surgery and premium cataract counseling, every patient receives individualized, thoughtful care.

01

Comprehensive ophthalmology

Evaluation and ongoing medical care for a wide range of eye conditions, from routine concerns to complex medical eye disease.

02

Glaucoma care

Diagnosis, long-term monitoring, and treatment of glaucoma—including eye drops, laser procedures, MIGS, and conventional glaucoma surgery.

03

Cataract surgery

Comprehensive evaluation and counseling on cataract surgery, including timing, lens planning, and coordination with glaucoma care when needed.

04

Premium IOL counseling

In-depth discussion of intraocular lens options tailored to visual goals and lifestyle—including an honest assessment of tradeoffs and realistic expectations.

05

Complex anterior segment

Management of advanced anterior segment conditions, combined surgical procedures, and lens-related surgical problem solving requiring specialized expertise.

06

Collaborative care

Close coordination with optometrists, primary care providers, and specialists to support patients with complex medical or ocular histories.
