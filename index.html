About

A physician who believes patients deserve clarity.

“I want every patient to leave their appointment better informed about their eye conditions than when they arrived.”

Siddharth Bhargava is a Board-certified, Fellowship-trained Ophthalmologist with expertise in glaucoma management, cataract surgery, premium intraocular lenses, and complex anterior segment care.

Dr. Bhargava grew up in the Bay Area and was fascinated by the high-tech environment of Silicon Valley. He was drawn to Ophthalmology after conducting Bioengineering research at UCLA that highlighted the importance of innovation in providing personalized and precise medical care. He returned to California in 2025 with his wife, Mansi, to be closer to their family and friends.

He has trained at institutions across the country and has consistently observed a profound lack of patient education at every stage of care. It is not infrequent that he encounters a patient treated for glaucoma for over ten years who doesn’t know what glaucoma is or why they are taking their drops. Thus, his approach to care is built on patient education: helping people understand not just what treatment is recommended, but why — and what to expect at every step.

Clinical Expertise

Areas of Focus

From routine comprehensive eye care to complex glaucoma surgeries — every patient receives thoughtful, individualized treatment.

01
Comprehensive Ophthalmology

Evaluation and ongoing care for a wide range of eye conditions, from routine diabetes and macular degeneration annual exams to complex medical eye disease and long-term monitoring.

02
Glaucoma Care

Screening, diagnosis, monitoring, and treatment of glaucoma — including medications, laser procedures, micro-invasive glaucoma surgery (MIGS), and conventional glaucoma surgery when appropriate.

03
Cataract Surgery

Comprehensive evaluation and counseling on cataract surgery timing, what to expect from the procedure, and ethically offers only intraocular lenses that he believes would be worth your time. He is fellowship trained in all FDA-approved MIGS procedures and uniquely positioned to offer the most personalized glaucoma surgery for your eyes at the same time as cataract surgery if both conditions are present.

04
Premium IOL Counseling

In-depth discussions of advanced intraocular lens options based on each patient’s lifestyle, visual priorities, and eye health — with an honest review of tradeoffs and realistic recommendations.

05
Complex Anterior Segment Care

Management of advanced anterior segment conditions such as intraocular lens repositioning, intraocular lens exchange, and iris reconstruction.

06
Collaborative & Coordinated Care

Close coordination with optometrists, primary care providers, and specialists to support patients with complex ocular or medical histories. He does not treat you only as a patient with an eye condition, but treats you holistically like a human being.

Glaucoma
